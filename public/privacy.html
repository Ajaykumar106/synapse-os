Privacy Policy

Synapse OS — AI Study Coach & Focus OS  ·  Effective Date: November 2026

🔒 Synapse OS operates 100% offline. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

1. What We Collect

Synapse OS does not collect any personal data. All information you enter — tasks, notes, routines, focus sessions, reminders — is stored exclusively on your own device in local JSON files.

No account creation is required. No email, name, or identity is ever requested or stored.

2. Local Data Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device:

  • Study tasks and to-do items (tasks.json)
  • Daily routine blocks (routine.json)
  • Personal notes and scratchpad entries (notes.json)
  • Focus session statistics (discipline.json)
  • Reminders and alarms (reminders.json)
  • Theme preference (browser localStorage)

None of this data leaves your device.

3. AI Processing

Synapse OS uses a local on-device AI model (Ollama) that runs entirely on your machine. Your chat messages and study context are processed locally — they are never sent to any cloud AI service, OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any third party.

4. Network Requests

Optional web-connected features (search, Wikipedia lookup, news) make outbound requests to public APIs when you explicitly use them. These requests are initiated by you and do not include any personal identifiers.

  • DuckDuckGo Instant Answers API (search)
  • Wikipedia REST API (knowledge lookup)
  • Google News RSS (optional news)

These services are subject to their own privacy policies. No personal data from Synapse OS is included in these requests.

5. No Analytics or Tracking

Synapse OS does not use:

  • Google Analytics or any analytics SDK
  • Crash reporting services
  • Advertising SDKs or trackers
  • Social media pixels or third-party cookies

6. Children's Privacy

Synapse OS is a study tool suitable for all ages. Because we do not collect any personal data, there is no special risk to children's privacy. The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 13.

7. Data Security

All your data is stored on your own device. You are in full control. To delete all app data, simply delete the data/ folder in the application directory, or uninstall the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the latest revision. We will not introduce data collection without updating this policy and notifying users.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach the developer at the contact information listed on the app's store page.
